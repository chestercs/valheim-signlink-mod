**Pictures, pixel art and labels on Valheim signs that every player sees, with or without the mod.**

## Features

- 🖼️ **Your own pictures on any sign.** Drop a PNG, JPG or GIF into a folder, pick it in game, put it up. Your clan's
  crest, a map of the base, a holiday photo, a famous painting.
- 👀 **Everyone sees it.** Your friends don't need the mod, and neither does the server. The pictures are ordinary
  signs saved in your world.
- 🐉 **Over 200 ready-made art pieces, 150 of them animated:** Valheim's creatures, cats and forest animals, scenes,
  runes, and kitchen icons for your chests.
- 🎞️ **Moving pictures.** The animated pieces move for every SignLink player, and your GIFs move wherever the GIF
  file is.
- 🧱 **From a chest label to a whole wall.** A big picture spreads over several signs and still looks like one.
- 🫥 **Transparent PNGs:** cut-out shapes sit right on the wall or the ground, with no box around them.
- 📐 **Any angle:** on a wall, on the floor, on the ceiling, tilted on an easel, turned any way you like.
- 💡 **Glowing or lit:** pictures glow in their own colours at night, or take the light of the scene.
- 🔤 **Labels in one click:** coloured text, blocks to paint a wall, symbols, patterns, even a QR code phones can read.
- ✏️ **Change it any time.** Select the sign again and everything you set is still there; Copy and Paste carry a
  picture to another sign.
- 🌍 **Language support:** Game default (follows your game's language), English, Magyar, 简体中文, Русский.
- 🎨 **Two looks for the window:** Classic (dark wood and gold) and Nordic (slate and amber).

## Three steps, and it's on the wall

Build a sign, press **Page Up** (or type `/signlink` in the chat), and the window walks you through it:

1. **1 Sign:** press *Select sign* and click your sign.
2. **2 Content:** pick an art piece, one of your pictures, or write a text. You see it on the sign right away (only
   you, for now), and the *Summary* tells you how big it is.
3. **3 Place:** size it, move it, tilt it, then press the button at the bottom. Now everyone sees it.

Walk around with the window open, and hold the **right mouse button** to look around.

**Your pictures** go into the `SignLink/images` folder next to your worlds (on Windows
`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\IronGate\Valheim\SignLink\images`); the *Open folder* button takes you there. Six
famous paintings and a galloping horse GIF are waiting in it to start with.

## Playing with friends

- **Nobody else needs the mod**, and the server needs nothing.
- **Built for shared worlds:** a picture is something every arriving player downloads, so on a server SignLink keeps
  them light: at most 300 KB on one sign and 600 KB within 100 m. The *Area* bar shows how much room is left, and
  *Multiplayer max* picks the best quality that fits. Single player has no limits at all.
- **Moving GIFs:** share the GIF file with your friends and it moves for them too.
- **Wards are respected.** You can edit a friend's picture wherever you have access.

## Good to know

- Tip: turn **Bloom** off in the game's graphics settings: glowing pictures look much sharper without it.
- Keyboard and mouse. A dedicated server does not need SignLink.
- A big picture takes a moment to appear when a player walks up to it.
- Players without the mod: E and then Enter on a picture sign opens the game's own editor, which cuts the picture
  (Escape leaves it alone). Hide the sign in a wall with only its picture showing, and there is nothing to press E on.
- **Uninstall:** remove it in the mod manager. Your pictures stay in the world as ordinary signs.

Every setting, the limits and the details are on [GitHub](https://github.com/chestercs/valheim-signlink-mod) and in
the [wiki](https://github.com/chestercs/valheim-signlink-mod/wiki).
